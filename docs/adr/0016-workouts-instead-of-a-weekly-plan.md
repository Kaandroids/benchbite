# 0016. Store workouts on weekdays instead of one weekly plan

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-22

## Context

The onboarding saves one weekly plan per user (`workout_plans`), with one session per training day
(`plan_sessions`) and its exercises (`plan_exercises`). The plan is read-only and the onboarding
cannot be repeated, so the plan can never change.

On the Workout tab, users will manage their own workouts. They will:

- see them;
- switch them on and off;
- keep unused ones in an archive;
- later create and edit them (#72), or have them generated by AI.

A workout like "Pull" should be one thing the user can move to another day, put on several days,
or keep without a day.

## Decision

- **A workout is one session** (`workouts`), with its exercises (`workout_exercises`).
- **Weekdays:** a workout has any number of weekdays, none included. A workout without weekdays
  can be done on any day, and is not planned for one.
- **Active flag:** several workouts can be active at once. The active workouts make up the user's
  week on the Today tab. Inactive workouts are kept in the archive.
- **Migration:** the plan's tables are renamed rather than copied, so existing rows keep their ids.
  Each session of a plan becomes an active workout on its weekday, and `workout_plans` is dropped.
- **The onboarding:** `complete_onboarding()` saves each session of the generated plan as an active
  workout on its weekday.
- **Writes:** for now, users may only switch the `active` flag of their own workouts. Creating,
  editing and deleting come with #72.

The plan builder keeps returning a plan of sessions (ADR 0010). It is only used by the onboarding.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep one plan per user and make it editable:** users could not keep a workout for later, mix
  their own with the generated ones, or put one workout on several days.
- **Programs that group workouts:** one more level to create, switch and explain. Active flags and
  weekdays on the workouts cover the same needs for now. Programs can be added on top later.
- **Only one active workout per weekday:** simpler to show, but people do combine short
  workouts, such as core after legs. The Today tab can show more than one.

## Consequences

- The app reads the week from the active workouts. A workout on two weekdays appears on both.
- Logged workouts (#34) will refer to a workout and its exercises, which now outlive any one plan.
- `profiles.training_days` keeps the onboarding's answer. The days actually trained follow the
  workouts from now on.
